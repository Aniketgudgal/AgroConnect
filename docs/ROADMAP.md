## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|-----------|
| Security breach | High | Medium | Regular audits, SSL, best practices |
| Database failure | High | Low | Backups, redundancy, recovery plan |
| User adoption | High | Medium | Marketing, UI/UX, user support |
| Farmer adoption | Medium | Medium | Training, support, incentives |
| Payment issues | High | Low | Multiple gateways, monitoring |
| Scaling challenges | Medium | Medium | Cloud infrastructure, optimization |
